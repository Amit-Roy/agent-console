# Asset sources

- `vendor/gsap.min.js` is GSAP 3.14.2, distributed under the [GSAP standard license](https://gsap.com/standard-license).
- Archivo, IBM Plex Mono, and Cormorant Garamond come from [Google Fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts). Their SIL Open Font License files are included in `fonts/`.
- The music and sound effects are original synthesized audio generated by `../scripts/synth.py`.
- The recordings show the real Agent Console interface on an isolated mock server with fictional conversations.
- The print palette and motion direction reference mexicat/pdoom; no footage or soundtrack from that repository is included.
